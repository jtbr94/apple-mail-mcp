A washed Grade 1 from the Gedeo highlands of southern Ethiopia. Heirloom selections grown on fertile red-brown clay under native enset, albizia, acacia, and millettia shade. Cherries hand-sorted at intake, disc-pulped, fermented under water for 36 to 48 hours, soaked a further 6 to 8 hours, then dried on raised beds for 6 to 7 days. USDA Organic certified.
