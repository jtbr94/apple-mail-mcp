About Sahkō

The art of origin.

Sahkō roasts single-origin coffee in small lots and publishes what it knows about each one: region, varietal, altitude, process, harvest, acidity, body, roast and lot number.

The collection is short on purpose. Six coffees, each chosen for a character that survives the roast and reads clearly in the cup.
